## How to read this

- **Implied multiple** = asking price ÷ earnings (EBITDA if disclosed, else SDE). Compared to the market band for that size/basis, plus an MRR premium of +1.5 turns when recurring-revenue language is present.
- **Below the band** is not automatically "good" — for a healthy MSP it usually signals owner dependence, customer concentration, churn, or one-off earnings. Treat it as a reason to dig, not a green light.
- **Price withheld** rows show the implied ask the disclosed earnings would support at market multiples.
- ⚠︎dup marks listings with identical price + cash flow (likely the same deal listed twice).
