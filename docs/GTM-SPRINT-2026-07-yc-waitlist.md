## Repositioning (decided 2026-06-23 — overrides the beachhead framing)

The thesis is the **company brain for any business**, per the YC summer ask and the 2026-06-22
D19 reposition ("one person or a company, the same brain either way"). A solo-photographer
*beachhead* is too narrow to carry that thesis, and an all-photographer waitlist would
*contradict* an "any business" pitch. **The composition of the waitlist is itself evidence for
the thesis** — so breadth is the goal, and photographer is just one persona among many.

We genuinely don't know who the customer is yet. That is an argument for **running specific
tests, not for messaging "everyone."** There is no audience called "everyone"; cold, a generic
message reaches no one. We get a *broad waitlist* through a *portfolio of specific messages*.

**Beachhead/wedge framing: dropped.**

## Positioning (two layers)

**Layer 1 — Thesis / umbrella (persona-agnostic, carries the vision):**
> "Whether you're a freelance photographer, a small pizza shop, a regional manufacturer, or a
> multi-national enterprise — Nibbin is the AI team that learns how you operate, and runs your

Breadth via concrete exemplars (solo → SMB → mid → enterprise = "the same brain at any scale").
Lives on the landing hero, About, YC pitch, and the product-led content. The §14.1 locked tagline.

**Honesty guardrail:** "multi-national enterprise" is a **vision** claim. Multi-user is roadmap,
not shipped (D19). Keep it as vision; do NOT promise enterprise features (multi-seat/SSO/admin)
anywhere in the funnel, and do NOT run discovery cuts targeting enterprise buyers we can't serve
in 35 days. Discovery tests run on solo→SMB tiers; enterprise stays the implied ceiling.

**Layer 2 — Acquisition hooks (specific, pain-led — also the discovery instrument):**
The exemplars become individual short-form cuts, each a specific person's pain:
- Solo creative (photographer/designer/videographer): "POV: 11pm, still answering inquiry emails."
- Local brick-and-mortar (pizza shop/salon/cafe): "POV: doing payroll + inventory after close."
- B2B / product (small manufacturer/wholesaler/Etsy seller): "POV: chasing POs and overdue invoices."
- Professional services (agency/consultant/coach/bookkeeper): "POV: your admin eats the billable hours."

Run them in parallel, tag each cut, instrument which converts. **The data picks the customer.**

## Why short-form video is still the bet

Only cold channel where reach is algorithmic, not owned. The channel Nibbin uniquely wins
(creatures animate; beige competitors can't follow). **The creature engine
(`packages/creatures`) renders the visual layer in code** — the "I have nothing to film"
bottleneck is a render call. Breadth doesn't change this; we just run more persona cuts on top.

**Honest feasibility:** modal outcome of cold accounts over ~30 days is **hundreds** of signups,
not thousands. Short-form→bio-link conversion is low single-digit %. Thousands is a breakout
outcome: possible, not plannable. **The YC app stands on the floor case** — hundreds of signups
+ the persona-conversion data + John's own diagnosis number — with a breakout clip as upside.

## Waitlist flow (email + one optional tap, now also the discovery instrument)

Never gate the email submit on extra info. Capture the conversion, enrich behind it.

1. **Passive signal (ask nothing):** per-cut UTM source, platform, referrer, timestamp, device.
2. **One optional inline tap — now spectrum-aware:** "What do you do?" chips:
   *Solo creative · Local business · Professional services · Product/maker · Larger company · Other.*
   One tap, skippable. **This is the customer-discovery readout** — it tells you who's signing up.
3. **Progressive profiling post-signup:** confirmation screen + first email ask 2-3 skippable
   questions (what you do all day, biggest time-sink, "would you do a free 14-day study?").
   Answer rate = demand signal; answerers = design-partner shortlist.
4. **Incentive (line-jump): deferred** — lowest-friction posture chosen. Revisit if profiling
   response is weak.

A/B the inline chip in week 1; cut if it bites conversion.

## Bio-link attribution (Day 1-3 build)

TikTok/IG bios are one static URL — no per-cut attribution without a router. Build
`nibbin.com/go?c=<cutId>` (stamps UTM, 302s to waitlist), or a Linktree-class page if faster.
Without it, per-cut UTM is fiction. Part of instrumentation task zero. **This is what makes the
persona-discovery test work** — it ties each persona cut to its signups.

**Engine:** short-form video, creature-native, TikTok (primary) + Reels + Shorts. **Post natively
+ staggered; no identical auto-cross-posts** (throttles new-account reach). Each cut CTA → `/go?c=`.

**Content is not just a render call.** Reach rides trending audio + native editing. Each clip =
engine render + trend/audio pass + human hook + caption. Skipping the trend/edit pass is the
beige-automation failure mode we're beating.

**Content pillars:**
1. **Product-led / thesis (the hero):** the creatures, the company brain, "an AI team that learns
   how you operate." Persona-agnostic. Carries the YC vision. Includes the user-zero arc + reveal.
2. **Persona-discovery cuts (the instrument):** the 4 pain-led persona hooks above, tagged + instrumented.
3. **Privacy flex:** "we built screen observation that *can't* phone home." Anti-surveillance.

**Capacity budget:** ~45 min/clip end-to-end. **Daily target; 4 clips/week hard floor.** "Daily"
slipping to 4×/week is not failure — quitting in the flat week is.

**The one durable automation — render→clip pipeline (Days 1-3, hard timebox):**
- Day-1 tool spike: **Remotion** (React/programmatic video; fits the React-rendered creatures) or
  **ffmpeg**; captions via Claude; **post manually + native** (no third-party auto-scheduler).
- **Semi-manual fallback:** export the creature moment from the engine, edit + caption by hand in
  CapCut, upload native. Acceptable; clips may then start Day 5-6.

**Cheap one-time levers:** directory blitz (BetaList, PH **Coming Soon**, Peerlist, Uneed,
microlaunch, There's An AI For That — Week 1, evergreen); build-in-public (X+LinkedIn, Claude
drafts from changelogs — seeds the YC founder narrative).

- **Video is hit-driven** → volume + multiple personas/formats + the reveal's warm seeding.
- **Cold week 1-2 looks flat** → expected; quitting early is the real failure.
- **New-account shadowban** → warm up, native + staggered, no identical cross-posts, link in bio only.
- **"Broad" decays into generic** → enforced by running specific persona cuts, never one generic message.
- **Enterprise over-claim** → "multinational" stays vision-only; no enterprise feature promises; no
  enterprise-targeted cuts.
- **Community ToS/ban** → 9:1 value, post as John, never automate, read each community's rules.
- **Pipeline build eats the sprint** → hard 3-day timebox + semi-manual fallback.
- **Reveal number unimpressive** → busy window; honest framing fallback.
- **YC app competes with finale** → named Days 28-35 workstream, not an afterthought.
