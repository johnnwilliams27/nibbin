# Triangle MSP Market Research

Market survey of IT managed service providers in the Raleigh–Durham–Chapel Hill (Triangle) area,
built 2026-08-07, for competitive analysis and acquisition scouting.

**Deliverable:** `Triangle-MSP-Market-Research.xlsx`

- **Target List** — 57 companies: tiered (A = prime succession-window targets, B = established/watch,
  C = verify, F = franchise, X = already acquired), with website links, employee bands + sources,
  formula-driven revenue AND sale-price estimates (assumptions adjustable on the Method sheet),
  ownership structure, owner/principal names, and clearly-labeled sale-signal inferences.
- **Small Shops (Roll-up)** — 25 micro-operators (1–5 people) rated High/Medium/Low as tuck-in
  targets by recurring-book quality and owner tenure, with micro-deal pricing norms.
- **Acquirers** — 23 buyers active in or near this market (PE platforms, strategics, local
  consolidators) with sponsors, NC deals done, appetite, and 2025-26 valuation benchmarks.
- **Precedent Deals** — 17 Triangle/NC MSP exits and transitions since 2016.
- **Excluded & Out of Market** — documented exclusions so the research isn't re-done.
- **Method** — assumptions, data-quality caveats, and suggested verification next steps.

All owner-age/sale signals are inferences from public facts (founding year, tenure, career start
dates) — never verified ages or stated intentions. Third-party revenue figures (ZoomInfo etc.) are
algorithmic estimates.
