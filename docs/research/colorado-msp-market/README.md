# Colorado MSP Market Research

Market survey of IT managed service providers across Colorado — Denver metro, Boulder,
Colorado Springs, Northern Colorado (Fort Collins/Greeley), and the Western Slope/mountain
resort corridor — built 2026-08-18, for competitive analysis and acquisition scouting. Same
schema as the companion Triangle (NC) research for direct comparison.

**Deliverable:** `Colorado-MSP-Market-Research.xlsx`

- **Target List** — 52 companies: tiered (A = prime succession-window targets, B =
  established/watch, C = verify, F = franchise, X = already acquired), with website links,
  employee bands + sources, formula-driven revenue AND sale-price estimates (assumptions
  adjustable on the Method sheet), ownership structure, owner/principal names, and
  clearly-labeled sale-signal inferences.
- **Small Shops (Roll-up)** — 34 micro-operators (1–20 people) rated High/Medium/Low as tuck-in
  targets by recurring-book quality and owner tenure.
- **Acquirers** — 27 buyers active in or near this market (PE platforms, strategics, local
  consolidators), with sponsors, confirmed Colorado deals, appetite, and 2025-26 valuation
  benchmarks. New Charter Technologies (Denver-HQ'd, Oval Partners-backed) is the home-market
  platform.
- **Precedent Deals** — 15 confirmed Colorado MSP transactions since 2015.
- **Excluded & Out of Market** — documented exclusions so the research isn't re-done.
- **Method** — assumptions, market snapshot by sub-region, data-quality caveats, and next steps.

## Key findings

- **Denver** is New Charter Technologies' home market, yet the platform has done only one
  confirmed local deal (Greystone, 2020) — while at least ten *other* national/regional
  platforms have separately bought Colorado MSPs since 2015.
- **Colorado Springs** carries a distinct defense/CMMC vertical (Space Force, Peterson,
  Schriever, Fort Carson); most local MSPs have no CMMC offering at all — a real gap.
- **The Western Slope is consolidating faster than its size would suggest** — four ownership
  events in Grand Junction alone since 2015, including Networks Unlimited itself acquiring
  out-of-state (a rival buyer, not just a target).
- **Northern Colorado (Fort Collins/Greeley) is the least picked-over sizable submarket** —
  only four firms meet NoCo-HQ + owner-operated + pre-2010 + ~10+ staff, and no confirmed
  acquisition of a Fort Collins/Loveland/Greeley-HQ'd MSP was found.
- **CommWest's November 2025 sale** (Grand Junction) is the best comparable for how a Colorado
  succession actually happens.

All owner-age/sale signals are inferences from public facts (founding year, tenure, career
start dates) — never verified ages or stated intentions. Third-party revenue figures
(ZoomInfo, RocketReach, D&B) are algorithmic estimates and several are demonstrably wrong in
this dataset (flagged inline).
