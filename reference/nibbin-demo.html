THE GROVEKEEPER
Your guide

Meet the Grovekeeper.

The elder of the grove runs your setup, your scan, and your daily check-ins — and explains every recommendation in plain language, including what it costs and what it touches.

Sets you up in minutes. A short conversation, not a configuration screen. The Keeper hatches your grove and connects your tools — you set what each Nibbin may do (Observe / Draft / Act) whenever you're ready.
Finds the busywork. It runs the 90-day scan, names what it finds in hours and dollars, and introduces the Nibbin for each job.
Has no hands — by design. The Keeper can never send, post, pay, or delete. It explains and delegates; only Nibbins you’ve approved do work, at the trust level they’ve earned.

While the study runs in the background, the Keeper keeps working with you in the foreground.

Privacy & security

Built like we expect to be audited. Because we want to be.

Observation software earns trust through architecture, not promises. These aren't policies we wrote; they're constraints we built.

P-01

Your device does the work

Capture, redaction, and first-pass analysis run locally. Raw screenshots never leave your machine. Only redacted, structured workflow events are synthesized — never pixels.

P-02

Passwords can't be captured

Secure input fields are flagged by your operating system itself. We suppress them at capture time, structurally — not by trying to spot them in screenshots afterward.

P-03

Excluded by default

Banking, health, password managers, and personal email are never recorded. Add any app or site to the exclusion list. Pause everything with one hotkey.

P-04

The study ends. Really.

Observation hard-stops on day 14. Raw capture is deleted after your map is built — and you can watch the deletion happen. Daily review lets you remove any block before it's processed.

P-05

Encrypted at rest, keyed to you

The local study archive is encrypted with keys held in your device's secure hardware. Malware on your machine finds ciphertext, not your work history.

P-06

Auditable, opt-in, yours

The capture client is open source — read exactly what it records. Contributing anonymized patterns to improve recommendations is a separate, explicit opt-in. Your data is never sold.

Your data is never sold and never used for ads.
No AI training on your data without a separate, default-off opt-in — not by us, not by our model providers.
Agent run logs keep 90 days by default — you can shorten it or wipe them anytime.
Connection tokens live in an encrypted vault; one click revokes everything.
