Plate 00 · Foreword

A field guide to Nibbin.

Nibbin is a grove: a warm, living place where small creatures grow into trusted help. Every visual decision serves that one idea. This guide is the single source of truth for the app, the landing page, the desktop Observer, emails, and anything else that wears the name — as binding as SPEC.md, and versioned the same way.

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Things grow into placeNothing slides for decoration. Entrances rise and settle; reveals sprout; celebrations bloom. If a motion can't be described with a growth verb, cut it.
Warm, not childishThe creatures carry all the whimsy. Everything around them — type, spacing, tables, copy — is calm, precise, adult. The contrast is the brand.
Trust is visibleStage pills, meters, journals, and logs are first-class visual citizens. We never hide the machinery of trust behind cuteness.
One celebration per momentDelight is rationed: first draft, evolutions, graduations, hatch-days. Everything else stays quiet so those moments land.

The signature element across all surfaces is the living creature — engine-rendered, idle-animated, present where work happens. When in doubt, spend personality there and discipline everywhere else.

Plate 0W · The Wordmark (frozen v1.0)

The living wordmark

"nibbin" in Bricolage Grotesque 800, lowercase, with the second i replaced by a sprout — tapered stem as the letter's body, two unfurling leaves as the dot. One living letter inside a bold ink word. The system is deliberately small: the wordmark is the identity; the only companion is the sprout extracted for favicons and app icons (always the same glyph, never a separate drawing). Values below were settled by iteration — they are law, not suggestion.

Construction (master = 56px, baseline y=70)

ElementValue
Composition"nibb" at x=8 · sprout glyph at gx = 8 + advance("nibb") + 0.5 + kern A · trailing "n" at gx + 17.5 + kern B
Kerns (the optical pair)Light: A=+4, B=−12 · Dusk: A=+5, B=−11 (irradiation wants looser air)
StemFilled taper 7.0→2.3, apex y=36.5 (≈60% of font stem weight). Dusk baseline overshoots to y=71.2; light does not
LeavesLeft tip (−6.5,30) · right tip (23.5,28.6) — exactly ascender height. Outlines 1.4 light (#44601F) / 1.2 dusk (#5F8F33). Overhang is load-bearing for the kerns — never trim
Color, lightLetters #23291A · stem #44601F · leaves #5B7C2E / #7FAF45
Color, duskLetters #E8ECDD · stem #9CC25B · leaves #9CC25B / #7FAF45

The six laws

1) Weight: organic elements at ~60% of font stem weight — heavier is a blob, lighter is a crack. 2) Line: nothing above ascender height. 3) One gesture: a single sprout, second i only; the first i keeps its plain dot. 4) Ground variants are real artwork — light and dusk differ in leaf weighting, kerning, and overshoot; two outlined files, never one recolor. 5) Leaves unfurl outward, never pray upward. 6) Exit, not entry: the word resolves into growth.

Use

Clear space: ≥1Λ on all sides, where Λ = the sprout's leaf-span (30 units at master). Sizes: wordmark ≥16px tall; below that, the extracted sprout mark only (favicons 16/32 always use the mark). Grounds: light artwork on Paper/Canopy/Shell/Understory; dusk artwork on Dusk and darker; never on mid-greens, photos, or gradients. Never: honey leaves, sprout on the first i, two sprouts, stretch/condense, rotation, dusk artwork on light, shadows or gradients in the letterforms, the sprout as a bullet point. Motion (the only two): the Growing on load (dotless stem sprouts, ≤900ms, once per session) and the Settle on hover (±4° once, 350ms); both yield to prefers-reduced-motion. Full construction record: reference/nibbin-logo-guide.html.

Plate 02 · Typography

Three voices, three jobs

A characterful display face for brand moments, a disciplined workhorse for everything functional, and a mono for data and labels. The display face is the one deliberate upgrade over the original system: Archivo alone was credible but anonymous; Bricolage Grotesque brings the slightly organic, hand-shaped warmth the creatures deserve — without tipping into toy.

DataIBM Plex Mono400 / 500 / 600Labels, eyebrows, stats, meters, timestamps, code, stage pills

  • Reserve the display face for moments worth a capital letter
  • Keep mono labels uppercase with wide tracking — they are the wayfinding
  • Use sentence case everywhere except mono labels
DON'T
  • Set paragraphs in the display face or in mono
  • Add new families or weights without amending this guide
  • Letter-space lowercase body text

Plate 04 · Creatures

The living signature

Engine v2: distinct silhouettes per species, shape language matched to personality, baked-in gesture and asymmetry, species-specific eye systems, and cel shading via clipped masses. The Keeper is canonical — one design, fixed moss-and-honey, identical in every account; it is the brand mascot. All art comes from the parametric engine (packages/creatures; reference: nibbin-creature-lab.html). Specimens below are engine-rendered — note the idle bob, the blink cycle, the egg's wobble. These behaviors ship with the art; a static Nibbin is a broken Nibbin.

Rules of the species

DO
  • Render through the engine — never hand-draw or trace
  • Give every instance unique gradient IDs (engine handles it)
  • Keep clear space ≥ ½ the creature's width on all sides
  • Show the earned stage truthfully — anatomy is the trust UI
DON'T
  • Stretch, rotate, flip, or recolor outside the eight families
  • Put a graduation cap on the Keeper, palette-shift it, or redraw it — one canonical Keeper, everywhere
  • Use creatures as error mascots for system failures (raised hand ≠ crash)
  • Crowd more than one celebrating creature per moment

Plate 06 · Motion

Things grow into place

Motion is the brand's body language. Four duration tokens, three easings, and a contract: every animation must be describable with a growth verb — rise, settle, sprout, bloom, perk. If the verb is "slide," "spin," or "bounce around," it doesn't ship.

Durations

TokenValueWhere
--t-micro160msHovers, presses, toggles
--t-base250msCard lifts, color shifts, small reveals
--t-pane350msTab panes, drawers, accordion
--t-grow650msEntrances, scroll reveals, ceremony beats
ambient2.8–22sCreature idle (bob 3.6s, blink ~5.5s, wobble 4.2s, float 4s, glow 2.8s), drifting leaves (17–22s)

Easings — live

--e-settle cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — arrivals: fast out, gentle landing
--e-pop cubic-bezier(.34,1.45,.64,1) — celebrations: a small overshoot, like a sprout springing up
--e-glide ease-in-out — ambient loops only

Entrance choreography (hero pattern)

BeatDelaySpec
Eyebrow → H1 → lede → CTAs → note50/140/240/340/440msrise 18px · --t-grow · --e-settle
Lifecycle stops (egg→graduate)550/720/890/1060mspopin scale .9 · --e-pop — the brand story told in motion
Scroll revealsstagger 70–90ms/childrise 16px, fire once, threshold .12
Scroll reveal

Grows into place

16px rise, settle easing, once only.

The contract

DO
  • Animate transform and opacity only
  • Fire reveals once; never replay on scroll-up
  • Stagger siblings 70–90ms so groups feel alive
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion with full feature parity
DON'T
  • Exceed one --e-pop celebration per moment
  • Run ambient elements above ~14% opacity
  • Animate layout properties (width, height, top)
  • Let any functional content depend on motion to appear
