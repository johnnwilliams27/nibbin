The Living Wordmark

The Nibbin wordmark is the name set in Bricolage Grotesque 800, lowercase, with the second i replaced by a sprout — its tapered stem standing as the letter's body, its two unfurling leaves serving as the dot. One living letter inside a bold ink word. This document is the single source of truth: construction, laws, color, spacing, prohibitions, motion, and assets. Values were settled by iteration and are not estimates.

Plate L0 · The mark

Primary wordmark

Two pieces of artwork, not one recolored file. The light and dusk versions differ in leaf weighting, kerning, and baseline overshoot — each correction earned against real optical effects (irradiation, value contrast). Neither may substitute for the other.

Plate L2 · The laws

Six laws, settled by iteration

Each was discovered by getting it wrong first. They govern this wordmark and every sibling variant.

  1. The weight law. Organic elements live at ~60% of the font's stem weight. Heavier reads as a blob; lighter reads as a crack in the word.
  2. The line law. Nothing rises above ascender height (y≈28.6 at master). The word stays level; growth obeys typography's rules the way a Nibbin obeys Agent School's.
  3. One gesture per word. A single living element. The first i always keeps its plain dot.
  4. Ground variants are real artwork. Light: moss-weighted leaves, tighter kerns (+4/−12), no overshoot. Dusk: leaf-weighted, looser kerns (+5/−11), +1.2 baseline overshoot. Two outlined files. Never recolor one into the other.
  5. Leaves unfurl outward, never pray upward. Lateral spread, gentle rise, tips at ascender height at most.
  6. Exit, not entry. The sprout lives on the second i — the word resolves into growth. It never moves to the first i.
Plate L3 · The mark beyond the word

One wordmark, one mark

The system is deliberately small: the wordmark is the identity. The only companion is the sprout extracted at large scale for surfaces where words can't survive — favicons, app icons, tiny avatars. It is the same glyph as the wordmark's i, never a different drawing.

Monochrome: all-ink (light grounds) and all-dusk-ink (dark grounds) wordmarks are permitted for engraving, embossing, single-color print — the sprout renders as solid silhouette. Never an all-green wordmark, and never #9CC25B shapes on light grounds without their dark outlines (1.88:1 against Paper — it dissolves; this failure created Law 4). Honey #E8C44A appears in the wordmark only as the Seed & Sprout lockup's dusk seed.

Plate L5 · Clear space & minimum sizes

Air and floors

Clear space unit Λ = the sprout's leaf-span (30 units at master ≈ 0.54× the wordmark's height). Keep ≥1Λ of empty ground on all four sides — no type, no UI, no creatures. The Grovekeeper may stand adjacent in product UI at exactly 1Λ; everything else keeps full distance.

Plate L6 · Grounds

Where it stands

Approved grounds, light artwork: Paper #F5F6F2, Canopy #FFF, Shell #FBF6E6, Understory #EAEDE3. Approved grounds, dusk artwork: Dusk #222B1B and darker. Forbidden: mid-value greens (moss/leaf backgrounds swallow the sprout), photography, gradients, any busy ground — use the Clearing badge there instead. When in doubt: if a ground would fail the style guide's text-contrast law, it fails the wordmark too.

Plate L7 · Prohibitions

Never

✕ no honey leaves — green only
✕ sprout never on the first i
✕ one sprout per word, never two
✕ never stretch or condense
✕ never rotate the wordmark
✕ never dusk artwork on light grounds

Also prohibited without illustration: drop shadows, gradients or textures inside the letterforms, animating any element except per Plate L8, attaching the sprout to any other word, and using the sprout glyph as a bullet point or decorative scatter.

Plate L8 · Motion

How it moves (sparingly)

Two sanctioned behaviors. The Growing (load/launch): the wordmark renders with its second i as a plain dotless stem, then the sprout grows from it — stem rises, leaves unfurl outward; ≤900ms, ease-out, at most once per session. The Settle (hover, optional): the leaves rock ±4° once, 350ms, breeze-touched. Both honor prefers-reduced-motion by not running at all. Nothing else animates — the wordmark is calm because the product is.
