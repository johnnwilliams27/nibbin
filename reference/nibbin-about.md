# About Nibbin

*Little creatures that grow up working for you.*

## What Nibbin is

Nibbin is an AI helper made for people who work for themselves. Instead of handing you a blank box and a thousand things you could do, it gives you a small grove of agents (we call them Nibbins) that learn how you actually work and then quietly take the busywork off your plate, one task at a time. You do not build them, wire them together, or babysit them. You raise them, and they grow into the job.

## The problem we set out to solve

Most AI tools can do almost anything, which is exactly why they end up helping so little. They are powerful and open-ended, and they leave the hard part to you: figuring out what to automate, setting it up, and keeping it running over time. They are also strangely impersonal. They rarely feel connected to you or to the way you run your business, and most of them have no idea what you actually do all day.

Off-the-shelf agents exist, but they are generic. Shaping one to fit your real workflows takes time, a fair amount of technical comfort, and ongoing maintenance that most solo owners simply do not have to spare. So you are left with a lot of capability and very little direction. You can do everything, and you still do not know where to start.

We think that is backwards. The whole point of help is to be guided.

## How Nibbin works

Nibbin understands you before it does anything for you.

It begins by studying how you actually work. It reviews how you have been working recently and observes your day-to-day, and from that it builds a clear, plain-language map of your real workflows: where your time goes, where the friction is, and where you stand to gain the most. You see this map for yourself. For a lot of people, that picture alone is the first time anyone has shown them exactly where their week disappears to.

Only then does Nibbin suggest a few agents matched to your actual work, not a generic menu. Each Nibbin starts as a student. It drafts, you approve, and it earns more responsibility only as it proves, over real tasks, that it gets things right. We call this Agent School. You always see what your Nibbins are doing, why they are doing it, and how accurate they have been. Nothing is a black box, and nothing graduates to working on its own until you have watched it earn the right.

## Personable, not just powerful

We built Nibbin to feel like something you actually want to check in on. Your agents are creatures you can see, each one growing through visible stages as it learns your business. They carry report cards. They celebrate the first time they handle something on their own. The personality is not decoration. It is how trust becomes legible: you can look at a Nibbin and understand, at a glance, how much it has learned and how much it has earned. That sense of affinity, of working alongside something rather than configuring a tool, is the thing most AI products are missing.

## Trust is the whole point

Inviting a piece of software to observe how you work is a big ask, and we treat it like one. The trust model is built into how Nibbin is made, not bolted on as fine print:

- **What Nibbin sees stays with you.** Screen observation happens locally. The captures it uses to understand your workflows do not leave your device.
- **Nothing acts without your approval until it has earned it.** Every new Nibbin drafts for you first. Autonomy is granted by you, gradually, based on proven accuracy, and you can pull it back at any time.
- **You can see, edit, and delete everything Nibbin knows about your business**, in plain language, whenever you want.
- **Privacy is the architecture.** The product was designed from the ground up so that the trustworthy thing and the convenient thing are the same thing.

## Who Nibbin is for

Nibbin is for the people running a business of one: freelancers, creatives, independent operators, anyone who is both the owner and the entire staff. It is built especially for people who know AI could help them but have never had the time, the technical background, or a guide to make it actually work. If you have ever felt that these tools were built for engineers and not for you, Nibbin is the answer to that feeling.

It is not built to be another open-ended platform for people who want to assemble their own automations. It is built to be the helper that does the understanding, the setup, and the steady work for you.

## Why we built it

We started Nibbin because the most capable software in the world was arriving at the same moment that the people who could benefit from it most, the ones working entirely for themselves, were the least equipped to use it. The gap was not capability. The gap was guidance, trust, and a little bit of warmth. Nibbin exists to close it.
