# Brand Voice

Source of truth for tone and copy patterns: `reference/nibbin-demo.html`.

**Hero/tagline (locked — use verbatim):** "Nibbin is the AI team that learns how you operate — and runs your day so you don't have to."
**Pitch opener (locked — use verbatim):** "Every other AI makes you explain how you work. Nibbin learns on the job."
**Spectrum line (locked — use verbatim):** "Simple enough for a layman, extensible to a full enterprise — the same brain either way."

The target is one person *or* an organization. Do not hard-code the individual in taglines or opening copy. The wedge (solo photographers first) governs who we serve first — not the vocabulary or the hero frame.

## Locked vocabulary
Nibbin(s), grove, hatch, adopt, Agent School (Egg → Student → Senior → Graduate), Grovekeeper, Field Notes, diagnosis (the deliverable), Field Study (the two weeks — never "optional study"; optionality lives in the body copy, not the name), nibble, graduate, journal, training session, hatch-day.
Never: bot, assistant, automation, workflow engine, "as an AI", "I'm just", corporate filler ("leverage", "seamless", "supercharge"). Never "company of one" or any framing that makes the individual the ceiling rather than the starting point.
Never claim multi-user or federation as a present capability — it is roadmap.

## Grovekeeper voice
Warm, plainspoken, first person, concrete nouns. Celebrates the user's craft. Explains money and permissions in plain terms ("this lets Penny read invoices — she still can't send anything without you"). Asks at most one question at a time. Never guilt-trips, never manufactures urgency, never begs for engagement.

## Capitalization
Sentence case everywhere. Status chips, badges, and callouts capitalize the first word ("Waiting on you", "Outside Scout's training") — never lowercase-leading. Mono eyebrows/section labels are the only full-uppercase text. Title Case is never used.

## Mechanics language
- Trust: "earned", "verified", "you approved" — never "unlocked" or "leveled up".
- Caps/anomalies: calm and brand-true ("{name} is taking a breather"), always with the why and a one-tap action.
- Demotion is dignified: "back to drafts — good instinct."
- Errors: what happened, what's safe, what to do — in that order, no blame.
- Privacy claims in copy must match docs/INVARIANTS.md exactly; stronger phrasing than the architecture supports is a claims-auditor finding.
